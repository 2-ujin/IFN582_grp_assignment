About This Project

janie._.bean is a family-based handmade seed bead project run by Janet and Queenie. The project name combines both of our names and represents the creativity we share.

We enjoy making colourful peyote and loom-patterned accessories by hand, with each piece carefully designed and made with love. What started as a time-killing activity has grown into a project we are excited to develop further.

With your support, we hope to purchase more seed beads, threads, tools, packaging, and other supplies. This will help us create new designs and improve our products.

Little as it is, we are grateful for every donation. Your support will help us build our little mother-and-daughter project.

Thank you for supporting janie._.bean!

About the Artist

QK

Queenie Kwok

Hi, this is Queenie, and I've always loved beading. For 12 years, I've been making seed beads with my mother, Janet, and now we've decided to take it a step further to janie._.bean.

For me, beading is a relaxing way to spend quality time with my mum. Every piece is made carefully by hand, with patience and attention to detail.

Through janie._.bean, I hope to share our creativity with more people at local markets, pop-up stores, and online. Thank you for supporting my work and our mother-and-daughter project!
